Group mentoring

One price. Everything included.

You are not buying a course to watch alone. You are joining a group mentored by a coach who has done the job, and who will answer the questions you actually have.

from $199 one time
  • Four preseason group sessions led by your mentor: standards, practice planning, community, and effective communication
  • Each session is an hour online: 45 minutes of instruction, 15 minutes for your questions
  • Open office hours with your mentor through the season, about twice a month
  • A short email from your mentor every Monday, often with a resource
  • Your mentor reaches out within 3 days of purchase
